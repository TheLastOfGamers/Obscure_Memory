How To Play
Objective
	⦁ Match all pairs of cards before the timer runs out.
	⦁ Earn stars and scores based on speed and combo streaks.
Game Flow
1. Level Selection
	⦁ Choose a level from the Level Selection Panel.
	⦁ Unavailable levels display “Coming Soon!” with a question mark icon.
2. Difficulty Selection
After choosing a level, select a difficulty:
	⦁ Easy: –10% score, +20% time, unlimited combo duration.
	⦁ Normal: Standard score/time , unlimited combo duration.
	⦁ Hard: +10% score, –20% time, combo timer (combo expires if not continued).
3. Gameplay
	⦁ Click/Tap to flip cards.
	⦁ If two cards match → they are destroyed, and points are awarded (with combo bonus).
	⦁ If they do not match → both cards flip back.
	⦁ On Hard difficulty, keep matching quickly to maintain your combo before it expires.
	⦁ The round ends when all pairs are matched OR the timer runs out.
4. Scoring & Stars
	⦁ Score is displayed at the top HUD.
	⦁ Earn up to 3 stars per round, based on score vs. target star score.
	⦁ Combo streaks increase your score multiplier.
5. Result Panel
At the end of a round, the panel displays:
	⦁ Score
	⦁ Stars Earned
	⦁ Options: Next Round, Retry, Menu
6. Progress & Records
	⦁ Progress, best score, and best time are saved automatically.
	⦁ Returning to a level resumes from your latest unlocked round.
	⦁ All progress can be reset via Settings.
7. Controls
	⦁ Flip Card: Click/Tap a card.
	⦁ Mute/Unmute: Toggle sound (icon changes accordingly).
8. Tips
	⦁ Try to memorize card positions for faster matching.
	⦁ On Hard mode, maintain speed to keep your combo streak alive.
	⦁ A countdown sound plays when the timer is nearly out – use it as a warning.
